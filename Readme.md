## 📖 Project Overview

The **GoldVista Retail Analytics** project is an end-to-end Business Intelligence solution developed to transform raw jewellery retail transaction data into meaningful business insights.

The project covers the complete analytics lifecycle, including:

* Data Cleaning & Validation
* SQL Business Analysis
* Data Modeling
* Interactive Power BI Dashboards
* Business Storytelling
* Executive Reporting

The solution enables management to monitor sales performance, branch operations, customer behavior, returns, old gold buy-back activities, payment trends, and salesperson effectiveness through interactive dashboards.

---

# 🎯 Business Objective

GoldVista Jewellers operates multiple retail branches with thousands of daily transactions.

Business stakeholders required a centralized reporting solution to:

* Monitor company performance
* Compare branch performance
* Analyze product sales
* Understand customer behavior
* Track returns and buy-back activities
* Evaluate salesperson productivity
* Support data-driven decision making

This project addresses these requirements through modern Business Intelligence techniques.

# 🔍 Key Business Insights

* 22K jewellery contributes the highest share of revenue.
* Revenue is concentrated among a few high-performing branches.
* Gold products dominate total sales.
* A large proportion of customers are one-time buyers.
* Returning customers contribute significantly to long-term revenue.
* Return rates remain relatively low across branches.
* Old gold exchange programs drive additional sales opportunities.
* Cash remains the primary payment mode, while digital payments continue to grow.
* Premium products with higher making charges generate better profitability.
